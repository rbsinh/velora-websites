Privacy and account controls

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Velora account and the account information associated with it. Follow the steps below to send a deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter the email address associated with your Velora account in the form below.
  2. Select Prepare deletion request. Your device will open your email app with a request drafted for you.
  3. Send the email to Velora support. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting the account.

The request is not sent automatically by this webpage. You must review and send the email yourself.

What data is deleted?

At present, Velora's backend is intended to store account sign-in information, such as the account email address and authentication-related records. When a verified request is processed, we will delete the Velora account and associated account records that we control.

Velora does not currently store food logs or meal history on its backend, so there are no stored food-log records to delete from that backend under the current setup.

Passwords: Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not available to Velora in readable/plain-text form.

Prepare an account deletion request

Enter the email address linked to your Velora account. This page uses your email app to prepare the request; it does not submit your email address to this website.

Use the email address you use to sign in to Velora.

Please allow up to 30 days for a verified request to be processed. Some information may be retained where legally required or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or resolving outstanding transactions or disputes. Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the applicable retention period.
