1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Velora handles information when you use the Android app. Velora is a personal food and activity tracker for adults. It is not a medical service.

Read it with the Terms of Use.

Back to top ↑

2. Information the app handles

  • Account: if you create an account, your email address and a password. The password is sent to Supabase Authentication so it can sign you in. Velora does not keep a copy of your password in its own database.
  • Profile and goals: details you enter, such as age, sex, height, weight, activity, and calorie or macro goals.
  • Diary: meals, portions, water, recipes, weight entries, streaks, and reminder choices.
  • Meal photos: a photo you take for a scan. The app compresses it, sends it for the estimate described below, and can save a thumbnail on the phone with the meal you choose to keep.
  • Steps and activity: if you connect Health Connect, the app reads steps, distance, and active calories so it can show today's movement and the step notification. A daily step total may be stored on the phone.
  • Device permissions: camera, notifications, and physical activity, only for the feature that needs them.
  • Support mail: if you use Feedback & Support, your email app opens a message to rbsinh96@gmail.com. We receive only what you choose to send.

Velora does not ask for your contacts, SMS, precise location, or advertising ID. There is no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK in the current app.

Back to top ↑

3. Where that information stays

The diary, profile, goals, reminders, appearance, and saved meal thumbnails are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded as part of sign-in.

These items leave the phone only in the cases below.

Back to top ↑

4. Meal scan

When you accept a photo for analysis, Velora sends that image to Google Gemini to suggest food names, amounts, and nutrition estimates. Your account email is not sent with that request.

You see the estimate and choose Select or Discard. Nothing is added to the diary until you select it.

We use the photo to produce that estimate and, if you save the meal, to keep a thumbnail on your phone. We do not use your photos to train a Velora model. Google's own terms apply to that processing. We do not control, and do not promise, how long Google keeps a request it has already received.

Back to top ↑

5. Account sign-in

Email sign-in is provided by Supabase Authentication. Supabase receives your email, password, and the session needed to keep you signed in. The session is stored on your phone so you stay signed in. The food diary is not stored in that account in the current version.

Back to top ↑

6. Health Connect

Health Connect is an on-device store from Google. Velora reads steps, distance, and active calories only after you grant those permissions. Active calories shown in the app or notification come from Health Connect, not from a Velora formula.

If you turn on export, Velora may write weight or exercise back to Health Connect. That export is off unless you enable it. Records already in Health Connect remain under your Google Health Connect controls.

Back to top ↑

7. How we use information

  • show and store the diary, goals, streak, weight, and steps you asked to track;
  • estimate a photographed meal;
  • sign you in and keep that session;
  • show the step notification and reminders you turned on;
  • answer a support email you send; and
  • protect the app against abuse and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information.

Back to top ↑

8. Who else receives information

  • Google Gemini — the meal photo and the prompt needed to identify foods and estimate nutrition, when you run a scan.
  • Supabase — email and authentication session, when you create an account or sign in.
  • Google Play and Health Connect — on your device, for install, optional purchases, and the fitness data you grant.
  • Your email app — only when you tap Feedback & Support.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is reasonably necessary to investigate abuse or protect the security of the app or its users.

Back to top ↑

9. Countries where processing can happen

The diary stays on your phone. A scan or sign-in can be processed outside India:

  • Google Gemini may process the photo on Google infrastructure;
  • Supabase hosts authentication for the Velora project on its infrastructure.

Those transfers happen only when you use the related feature.

Back to top ↑

10. How long information is kept

  • On the phone: diary entries, profile, settings, and saved meal thumbnails stay until you delete the entry, use Delete all local data, or uninstall Velora.
  • Account: Supabase keeps the email and sign-in record until you ask us to delete the account or we delete it.
  • Scan requests: we do not keep a separate cloud copy of the meal photo. The thumbnail you save stays on the phone with that meal.
  • Support email: we keep a message only as long as needed to answer it and handle any follow-up.
Back to top ↑

11. Deletion

You can remove the on-device diary, profile, and saved photos with Delete all local data in the app. Uninstalling Velora also removes the app's private storage on that phone.

To delete the sign-in account, email 96rbsinh@gmail.com from the account address and ask for account deletion. We will delete the Supabase authentication record. That request does not erase a diary that exists only on a phone we cannot reach. Use Delete all local data or uninstall for that copy.

We may keep a record where the law requires it, or where it is needed for security, fraud prevention, or a dispute.

Back to top ↑

12. Your requests

Subject to Indian law, you can ask what account information we hold, ask for a correction, withdraw consent for a permission-based feature, or ask for deletion. Email 96rbsinh@gmail.com and include the email on the account. We may ask you to confirm that the request is yours.

You can refuse or later turn off camera, notifications, physical activity, and Health Connect access in Android settings. The related feature will then stop or stay limited. The rest of the on-device diary still works.

13. Children

Velora is for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly create an account for a child. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email 96rbsinh@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

14. Security

The app talks to Gemini and Supabase over HTTPS. The diary is stored in app-private storage, protected by your device lock and Android's file encryption. Sign-in tokens stay on the device.

No internet service is perfectly secure. Protect your phone and your Velora password. Do not send your password in a support email.
