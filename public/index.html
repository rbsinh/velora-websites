Three ways to log the same meal

Use whichever is in front of you. Each one ends in the same place: an estimate you can check, then a line in today’s diary.

Say it in a sentence

Write the meal the way you remember it. Velora turns that into calories, protein, carbs, and fat.

Photograph the plate

A photo can stand in for typing. You see the suggestion and choose whether to keep it.

Scan a barcode

Point the camera at a packaged food. Review the match, or enter the food if the code is not recognized.

Chat

Meals, written the way you remember them

A short sentence is enough to start. The estimate shows the calories and the macros, and the words you typed can stay with that entry.

Photo

A photo when you would rather not type

Point the camera at the meal. Velora suggests a name, an amount, and the calories. Nothing is added until you keep it.

Barcode scanner

Packaged food, without typing the label

The scanner reads the barcode on the packet. Velora shows the food and the calories so you can check them before they join the day. If a code is not recognized, you can still add that food yourself.

Step counter

Today’s steps, next to today’s meals

Set a daily step goal and watch the ring fill. Velora can read the count from Health Connect, and you can type the steps yourself when you want to. The last 7 days and the last 30 days stay on the same screen.

The day, kept in one diary

Calories are only the start. The same home screen holds the rest of what you chose to track.

Protein, carbs, and fat

Each saved meal adds to the day’s macros, next to the calories you have left.

Water

Log cups through the day and keep a target in view.

Streak

Logged days stay marked, so a quiet week is easy to see.

Weight

Record weight beside the meals, without a second app.

Reminders

Optional nudges for meals, water, and the habits you turn on.

On this phone

The diary, photos you save with a meal, goals, and reminders stay in the app on your phone. Signing in prepares an account. It does not, by itself, upload that diary.
