3. What Velora Does

Velora is a personal food and activity tracker. Depending on the features you use, it lets you:

  • log meals, portions, water, and saved foods;
  • photograph a meal and receive an estimated name, portion, calories, and macros;
  • set a daily calorie goal and view a weekly summary and logging streak;
  • record weight;
  • read steps, distance, and active calories from Health Connect when you allow it;
  • show an optional ongoing step notification; and
  • set meal, water, and other reminders, and choose a light, dark, or system appearance.

Your food diary, photos stored with meals, weight, goals, and reminder settings stay on your phone. Signing in creates an account. It does not, by itself, upload your diary to our servers.

Features can change. A feature shown in a test build may later move to a server, be removed, or be offered only after you confirm an upload.

4. Estimates, Not Measurements

Meal photos are analysed with an automated system. The current build sends the photo to Google Gemini to suggest foods, amounts, and nutrition estimates. You choose whether to save the result.

Those results are estimates. A photo cannot reliably show exact ingredients, oils, sauces, brands, cooking method, or portion size. Values can be incomplete or wrong, including for water or foods with little or no energy. Check labels, recipes, restaurant information, or a qualified professional when the number matters.
